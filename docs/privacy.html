FOLDLINE · Railwork Edition

Privacy Policy

A clear account of your information and your choices.

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy applies to FOLDLINE — Railwork Edition, including the Android app with package name com.app.foldlinerailworkmobile, its iOS edition where available, and its browser edition. The developer maintains the project under the GitHub account ayjays132. For privacy questions or requests, contact hi@showweld.com.

The essentials

Your railway progress and settings are stored on your device. Playing does not require a FOLDLINE account. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay saves, and the release covered by this policy does not request or display advertisements. Optional platform game services, when available and connected, have separate data handling described below.

1. Information kept on your device

The game stores completed journeys, route scores, collected stamps and seals, workshop upgrades, earned currency, dispatch counts, achievement progress and your preferences, including sound, music, motion and other gameplay settings. These records let you continue your railway and retain your choices.

These records use the app's local storage, or your browser's storage when playing the browser edition. We do not automatically upload your railway save to a FOLDLINE server. Device or operating-system backup and restore may retain app data according to your device settings and the backup provider's policies. This is separate from a FOLDLINE cloud-save service.

2. Information we do not ask for

Normal gameplay does not ask for your name, email address, telephone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. The game does not include developer-operated behavioral analytics, Firebase Analytics, or a developer-operated remote crash-reporting service. We do not sell your personal information or share gameplay records with advertising companies.

Your app store, operating system or platform provider may independently process purchase, installation, security or diagnostic information under its own settings and privacy policy. We may receive aggregate store performance information supplied through the developer console.

3. Optional platform achievements

Some editions or updates may offer Google Play Games on Android or Apple Game Center on iOS. These services are used only in builds where they are configured. The in-game honours collection also works locally without a platform connection.

When a supported service is connected, the game uses your platform sign-in status and platform player identifier to associate reports with the correct player. It sends achievement identifiers and earned progress or completion percentages to that platform. The platform may also process technical information necessary to authenticate you and operate its service under its own privacy policy. The game does not receive your Google or Apple password, and does not send your complete campaign save to these achievement services.

Platform achievements are separate from cloud saves. Disconnecting a platform or clearing the local game does not necessarily delete achievements already stored by Google or Apple. Manage that information through the platform account's game-data and privacy controls.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising and purchases

Live advertising is disabled in the release covered by this policy. Although advertising integration code may be included in the app, the release does not initialize an advertising session or request ads. Any locally stored advertising preference or age-band choice from an earlier test stays on the device; an age band is not a date of birth.

We will update this policy and provide any required notices and choices before enabling advertising or introducing additional data collection. Store payments, if applicable to obtaining the app, are handled by the store; FOLDLINE does not receive payment-card details.

5. Save exports and imports

If you choose to export or share a save, the exported file contains your railway progress and preferences. You control where that file goes and who receives it. Importing a save reads the file or text you select to restore progress. We do not automatically receive a copy. Copies you place in email, cloud storage or other apps are subject to those services' policies.

6. Support and privacy requests

If you email hi@showweld.com, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send, such as your message, device details, screenshots or a save file. We use it to respond, troubleshoot and handle your request. Please do not send passwords, signing keys, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Email providers process correspondence to deliver and store support messages. Public GitHub issues are visible to others, so use email for private requests. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information you have provided, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise applicable rights; we may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify and fulfill the request.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local railway progress: retained until you reset it, clear the app or browser data, or remove it through your device. In the game, open Settings and choose New railway, then confirm, to reset railway progress. To remove all local preferences and stored data on Android, use your device's app settings to clear FOLDLINE's storage. On iOS, delete the app rather than offloading it. In a browser, clear site data for the address used to play.
  • Backups and exported files: delete these separately wherever you stored them. A device backup may restore data unless you also manage that backup.
  • Platform achievements: retained and managed by Google or Apple under their policies and account controls. Clearing FOLDLINE's storage does not erase a platform's records.
  • Support correspondence: kept only as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by email; we will explain any information that must be retained and why.

There is no separate FOLDLINE account to delete. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase a save that exists only on your device.

8. Children and families

The local game can be played without entering personal information. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through gameplay. Platform accounts and family controls are governed by Google or Apple. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us to request its review and deletion.

9. Security and international services

We limit the information handled by the game and rely on device storage protections and the security of the services you choose to use. No storage or communication method is completely secure. Protect your device and any save files you export.

Google, Apple, GitHub and email providers may process information in countries other than your own according to their policies and applicable safeguards.

10. This policy website

This static page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or forms. GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address, browser details and access logs, to host and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy policy.

11. Changes and contact

We will revise this page when the game's data practices change and update the date above. Material changes will be accompanied by additional notice or consent where required.
